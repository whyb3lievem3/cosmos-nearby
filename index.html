Bring the classroom
to the cosmos.

Our lecture hall seats 120 guests beneath a full-dome planetarium ceiling. Every seat offers an unobstructed view of the speaker stage and the projected sky above.

Real science.
Human stories.

Each speaker is an active researcher — no actors, no scripts. They share their own discoveries, mistakes, and breakthroughs in plain language.

Good questions.

Are the lectures really free?

Yes. Aphelion is funded by grants and donations. Registration is required only to manage seat capacity.

Can I attend online?

Selected lectures are broadcast live. Register with your email and we'll send you the streaming link 30 minutes before the event.

Is it suitable for children?

Absolutely. Our speakers adapt their explanations for all ages. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.
